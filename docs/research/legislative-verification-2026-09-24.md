# Mati legislative references — 24 September 2026

## Scope and evidence standard

Replaced the five fabricated placeholder rows (including their dates) with three documented references. This is a partial collection, not a certified legislative register. No 2025–2026 measures were verified for inclusion. This is a research gap, not evidence of zero legislative activity.

The PNA article contents were available through web search indexing. Direct page retrieval returned errors in this research environment. Verification therefore means that the indexed government report identifies the measure's number, series year and subject; it does **not** mean that a signed instrument was inspected. Every published row carries the source URL, publisher, check date and `government-report-reference` status. Session dates, official titles and full-text URLs are null. Summaries are editorial paraphrases, translated into English, Filipino and Cebuano.

## Legal explanations

- [Republic Act 9408, Mati city charter](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/7810), sections 17–21: city legislative powers, provincial review, and effectivity. [Lawphil text](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2007/ra_9408_2007.html) also provides searchable statutory text.
- The charter's provision on the Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod provides for certified public records on request, subject to prescribed fees, and records of passage/publication.
- [Republic Act 7160](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/53542), sections 305(a) and 306(b): local appropriations require an ordinance or law. Removed “Appropriation” as a generic resolution category and replaced it with development planning.
- Removed the unqualified claim that all resolutions have no legal effect. The city charter expressly addresses resolutions approving development plans and public investment programs.
